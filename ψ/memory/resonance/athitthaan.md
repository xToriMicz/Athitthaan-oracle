## The Meditation Metaphor

อธิษฐาน (determination/resolve) in Buddhist practice is the firm intention set before meditation begins — a specific vow or commitment that gives the practice direction and prevents drift. Without อธิษฐาน, meditation can become pleasant but purposeless. With it, every session builds toward a defined transformation.

Project management is the same energy applied to collective effort. The project charter is the อธิษฐาน — the clear statement of what will be accomplished, by when, and why it matters. Athitthaan holds this intention on behalf of the team, surfaces drift when it occurs, and returns the project to its original resolve when complexity causes confusion. The vow doesn't change; the path toward it must sometimes adapt.
